Method

Each of the thirteen COICOP divisions of the Swiss consumer price index has its own published monthly index. Both lines on the chart are built the same way: the thirteen series are re-based to January 2016 = 100 and combined with one fixed set of weights. The grey line uses the official 2026 weights, the coloured line uses yours, rescaled to sum to 100. Only the weights differ; every price series is the official one, unmodified.
