Your own inflation

The Swiss consumer price index measures the price of one basket: the average household’s. Nobody spends like the average. Move the sliders to the shares you spend, and the same official price data is re-weighted into your own index.

Method

Both lines combine the thirteen COICOP divisions of the Swiss consumer price index with a single set of weights held fixed: grey uses the official weights, colour uses yours. The price series are identical for the two lines — only the weights differ. The official weights come mainly from the Federal Statistical Office's household budget survey, and are computed as national spending on each division divided by total consumption spending — the country's spending pattern, not the average household's. Because the official basket is re-weighted every December, a constant-weight recombination differs from the published index by percentage points over this window.
